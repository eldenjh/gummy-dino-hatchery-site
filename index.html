Gummy Dino Hatchery

Tap the egg. Out pops a gummy dinosaur.

A gentle 3D egg-hatching game for children aged 3 to 7. Every egg cracks in its own way, and every dinosaur inside is a glossy, see-through, wobbly gummy sweet that loves to be poked, tickled and squished.

Coming soon to the App Store

iPhone & iPad · iOS 16.4+ · Works offline · No ads, no accounts, no purchases

What's inside

Eight candy eggs

Meadow, jungle, frost, ember, golden, rainbow, spotted and a mystery egg. Each one needs a different number of taps, and the cracks grow with every tap until the shell bursts.

Twenty gummy dinosaurs

From Common to Mythic: sugary, frosty, glowing, golden and rainbow gummies of six kinds of dinosaur, each with its own name.

My Nest

Every dinosaur your child finds is kept in their nest. Tap one to bring it back and play: tickle it into a giggle dance, squish it and watch it jump, or tap the floor to make it hop over.

Made for little hands

No timers, no losing, no reading needed. Soft sounds and gentle haptics, big friendly buttons, and a swipe in from the edge of the screen first only shows the system handle, so a child is less likely to leave the game by accident.

Privacy, stated plainly

  • The app never connects to the internet. Everything it needs is inside it.
  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no purchases, no links out of the app.
  • It asks for no permissions — no camera, no microphone, no location.
  • The dinosaur collection is saved only on your device. Read the full privacy policy.
